EXPERIMENTAL HISTORICAL ADJUSTMENT

Test the gap between sensors.

The question is narrow. Can MODIS counts better approximate the combined MODIS–VIIRS record when an entire year is withheld from fitting?

44.8% lower daily MAE

Full study window, 4 km grid, Terra+Aqua input. Across 13 held-out years, the mean absolute error fell from 12.95 to 7.15 active cells per day. Every held-out year improved. The target is the observed cell union, so this measures internal agreement rather than independent fire-detection accuracy.

How the model works

For each grid size and input type, fit a multiplier through the origin on daily active-cell counts. Fit one slope for all seasons and one for each calendar quarter. Shrink each quarterly slope toward the all-season value when it has few active-input days.

global slope = sum(input × target) / sum(input²)
quarter weight = active input days / (active input days + 30)
quarter factor = max(1, weight × quarter slope + (1 − weight) × global slope)
estimated daily union = observed input cells × quarter factor

The target is the union of retained MODIS Terra+Aqua and VIIRS SNPP cells. Separate Terra-only models support 2001–2002. Combined MODIS models support 2003–2011. Final factors use all 2013–2025 training years; validation predictions use only the other 12 years.

Whole-year validation

All dates from the test year are excluded from fitting. Thirteen folds cover 4,748 days. Zero-observation days remain in the test. MAE is mean absolute error in active cells per day. The baseline is the unadjusted input count.

Checks beyond the headline number

We also tested a seasonal affine model and smaller case-study areas. Improvements were less consistent in those areas. The deployed adjustment is therefore restricted to the full study window. Candidate results are included in the repository rather than hidden.

A separate sensitivity test excludes all 211 UTC dates covered by listed NASA MODAPS Suomi NPP outage periods within the training span. At 4 km, MAE improvement remains 45.6% across 4,537 retained dates, with improvement in all 13 years. This exclusion is a coarse date mask, not a measurement of local cloud cover or observation availability.

A backward-transfer check fits 2018–2025 and tests 2013–2017. The 4 km Terra+Aqua adjustment reduces daily MAE by 44.2% in that earlier held-out period. This provides a limited test of transfer toward earlier years, although it cannot validate the pre-VIIRS period.

What the error ranges mean

Daily estimates use the 5th and 95th percentiles of held-out daily signed residuals. Monthly estimates use residuals of whole-month sums, so their ranges are not obtained by adding daily bounds. Bounds are clipped at zero and expanded to include the point estimate. They are descriptive error ranges, not guaranteed prediction intervals. Input counts outside the training range are marked in the downloadable calibration artifact.

What remains unproven

There is no VIIRS record before 2012 with which to validate these historical estimates. Applying the overlap-era relationship to 2001–2011 assumes it transfers across changes in fire behavior, viewing conditions and archive processing. The input and target also share MODIS observations. Error reduction does not demonstrate an equivalent reduction in missed real fires.

The model cannot reconstruct missing fire locations. No estimated cells are added to the map or raw-detection CSV. It does not estimate burned area, ignition counts or future fire risk. A multiplier gives a zero point estimate when its input is zero; this does not establish that fire was absent.

Reproduce and inspect

See the source repository for preparation, calibration, all candidate results and tests. The complete calibration artifact (gzip JSON) contains fitted factors, every yearly fold, historical daily and monthly estimates, and empirical ranges. The NASA outage table supplies the sensitivity mask.
