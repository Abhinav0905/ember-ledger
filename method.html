DATA & METHODS

What the record can tell us.

Ember Ledger combines an observed fire calendar with a separate experimental estimate of earlier activity. The distinction stays visible in the app and its downloads.

01 · Data and coverage

The atlas contains 1,086,752 retained observations from 39 NASA FIRMS United States annual files. MODIS covers 2001–2025. VIIRS Suomi NPP covers 2012–2025. The study window is 125°W–117.7°W and 36.6°N–42.8°N, centered on Northern California and including parts of neighboring states. The latest included date is December 31, 2025. No 2026 or live observations are included.

Inputs retain type 0 (presumed vegetation fire), MODIS confidence ≥30 and VIIRS nominal or high confidence. These are separate product filters, not equivalent confidence scores. MODIS has nominal 1 km pixels; VIIRS has nominal 375 m pixels. MODIS includes Terra and Aqua when available. The original version fields vary across annual files. We publish those values and hashes below rather than label all years as one collection.

02 · One observed cell per UTC day

Observation coordinates are assigned to a 2 km equal-area cylindrical grid anchored at 125°W and 36.6°N, using a spherical Earth of radius 6,371.0088 km and a standard parallel of 39.7°N. The 4 km and 8 km grids nest the 2 km cells. For a selected rectangle, base cells are included when their centers fall inside it.

A combined cell is active when either sensor has at least one retained observation on that UTC day. Shared cells indicate spatial agreement within a day; the observations can be hours apart. This union removes repeated counting at the chosen scale. It does not correct sensor sensitivity, missing coverage or differences in overpass time.

03 · Calendar, colors and seasonal reference

Monthly active-cell days are the sum of daily active-cell counts. A cell can contribute once each day, so these totals do not count separate fires or measure burned area. The heatmap shares a logarithmic color scale across all displayed years. The daily calendar can color by count or by the number of cells above its seasonal reference.

The reference uses 2017–2024. For each selected date, it gathers the same calendar date ±7 days in those reference years, excludes the selected year, then takes the nearest-rank 95th percentile. February 29 uses February 28 as the anchor in non-leap years. Dates outside the reference years are excluded. This gives 105 comparison days for August 5, 2021. Reference years stay fixed when the archive is extended.

Days with no retained observations contribute zero, although missing observations and clouds are not modeled. A diamond means the observed union exceeds the reference. The comparison is retrospective and can use later years. Flags are unavailable for MODIS-only years. The 2012 record is a transition year and may have incomplete VIIRS coverage.

04 · An experimental estimate for 2001–2011

The adjusted-history view applies a seasonal count adjustment only to the full study window. It estimates the combined-observation count from MODIS. It does not infer individual hotspot coordinates. Terra-only observations supply 2001 and all of 2002; Terra+Aqua supply 2003–2011. The heatmap marks estimated months with ~ and EST. The map, cell evidence and ordinary CSV exports continue to show observed data.

Separate models are fitted for each grid size and early-era input. We tested them by leaving out one complete year at a time across 2013–2025. Historical estimates assume that the overlap-era relationship also applies to earlier years. That assumption cannot be checked against VIIRS before 2012. See the validation results, formulas and limitations.

05 · Checks on the original Dixie example

On August 5, 2021, the Dixie Fire study area contains 999 retained MODIS detections and 3,755 VIIRS detections. At 4 km they occupy 98 union cells, including 76 shared cells, against a reference of 15.

06 · Downloads and reproducibility

Choose a date range for daily analysis, including zero-observation days, or download retained source observations with original coordinates, acquisition times, confidence and version fields. Source files use the same base-cell-center area rule as the map, so the raw row totals match the displayed detection totals. Added source and grid columns are labeled explicitly. A separate download contains modeled full-region totals for 2001–2011, regardless of the selected custom area or dates. SVG export provides a labeled chart of the observed daily counts.

The GitHub repository includes the Python download and preparation pipeline, calibration experiment, tests, app code and rebuild instructions. No API key or LLM service is required to run the site.

07 · Limits and next steps

Clouds, smoke, viewing geometry, overpass schedules, processing changes and outages affect the record. Positive-detection files do not say whether a satellite had a clear view. An empty cell can still contain fire. Case-study rectangles are study areas, not incident perimeter masks, so detections are not individually attributed to the named fire.

Historical adjustment reduces disagreement with a combined observed reference for this fixed region. It does not make the sensors interchangeable everywhere. The reference shares MODIS inputs with the predictor and is not independent ground truth. Extending the model to custom areas, adding observation-coverage masks and testing against independent measurements remain future work. A future 2026 update must label the data cutoff and any change between archive and near-real-time products.
