DATA & METHODS

How the grid works.

MODIS and VIIRS observe the same landscape with different pixel sizes, overpass schedules and detection sensitivity. Ember Ledger gives their retained observations a common unit: one active cell per UTC day.

01 · Which observations are included?

We use the NASA FIRMS country archive for the United States from 2017 through 2024. MODIS Collection 6.1 includes Terra and Aqua at nominal 1 km. VIIRS uses Suomi NPP at nominal 375 m. Inputs are clipped to 125°W–117.7°W and 36.6°N–42.8°N, a window centered on Northern California that includes parts of neighboring states. We retain archive type 0 (presumed vegetation fire), MODIS confidence ≥30 and VIIRS confidence nominal or high. The confidence filters are product-specific; they are applied independently.

02 · What counts as one active cell?

Each observation is assigned by its coordinate to a 2 km equal-area cylindrical grid anchored at 125°W and 36.6°N, using a spherical Earth and 39.7°N standard parallel. For a chosen area, we keep base cells whose centers fall inside the rectangle. A 4 km or 8 km view nests the retained 2 km cells into bigger cells. A combined cell is active if either sensor detected activity that UTC day. A cell seen by both means spatial agreement within the UTC day; the overpasses can be hours apart.

The cell union removes repeated observations at the chosen spatial scale. It leaves differences in sensitivity and sampling visible. The export lists the inputs and sensor agreement for each displayed cell.

03 · What makes a day unusual?

For each day, compare its combined cell count with the nearest-rank 95th percentile of counts on the same calendar date ±7 days across the other archive years. Feb 29 uses Feb 28 in non-leap reference years. Days outside the archive or within the selected year are excluded. Days with no retained observations contribute zero. The calendar marks a day when its count exceeds that reference. This is a retrospective screen. The reference can include years after the date being investigated and adjacent comparison days are correlated.

05 · Limits

Clouds, revisit timing, sensor sensitivity and occasional disruptions can reduce detections. This build uses positive detection files. A zero therefore means no retained hotspot observations in the archive for that date and area. It gives no assurance that a satellite had a clear view or that fire was absent. The atlas reports cell occupancy rather than burn area, ignition counts, incident attribution or forecasts.
